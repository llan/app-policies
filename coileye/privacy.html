CoilEye Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

CoilEye is an Android app that shows the picture from a USB inspection camera, endoscope or webcam plugged into your phone or tablet. No internet access. Your photos and videos never leave your phone. The developer does not collect, receive, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. CoilEye:

  • does not have the Android permission to use the internet (INTERNET), so it cannot send anything over the network;
  • has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash-reporting software;
  • has no accounts or sign-in;
  • does not use your location, microphone, contacts, or advertising ID.

Permissions it uses

  • Camera. Android requires this permission to show a USB camera's picture. The picture is used only on your device, to show it on screen and to save the photos and videos you choose to take. The app only opens external USB cameras, not your phone's own cameras.
  • USB access. Android asks you to allow the app to use the USB camera you plug in.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only). To save photos and videos to your Pictures and Movies folders.
  • Google Play billing. Used only when you choose to buy or restore the optional one-time Pro unlock.

What stays on your device

Photos and videos you take are saved as ordinary files in Pictures › CoilEye and Movies › CoilEye, and PDF reports in Documents › CoilEye. Notes you add, marked-up copies' edit layers, report drafts and the app's settings (such as the camera mode you picked) are kept in the app's own storage on the device. The developer cannot see any of it. Photos, videos and reports stay on your phone if you uninstall the app; the app's own storage is removed.

No backup of app data

CoilEye turns Android's app backup off. Its notes, settings and other app data are not copied to Google's cloud backup and are not transferred when you set up a new phone. Photos, videos and reports are ordinary files in your shared folders; whether those are backed up depends on the other apps and settings you use (for example a gallery app's own backup), not on CoilEye.

Things you choose to share

The app never shares anything by itself. If you tap Share on a photo, video or PDF report, or use "Share camera details", Android's share sheet opens and you choose the app or person to send it to. "Share camera details" is a short plain-text description of your USB camera and phone model that you can read before sending; it contains no serial numbers, photos or videos.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and tells the app only whether the unlock is owned, so it can restore it on a new phone signed in to the same Google account. The developer receives no payment card details from the app. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is a tool for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact shown on CoilEye's Google Play Store listing.
