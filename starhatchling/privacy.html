Star Hatchling Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Star Hatchling is a space shooter game made for young children. It does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game:

  • does not ask for any Android permissions, and has no internet access;
  • has no accounts, sign-in, chat, or ways to talk to other people;
  • has no ads and no in-app purchases;
  • has no analytics, tracking, or third-party software that collects data.

What stays on the device

The game keeps its progress (player profile names and pictures chosen in the game, stars, levels finished, and items bought with in-game stars) in a save file on the device. This file never leaves the device through the game, and the developer cannot see it.

If Android's own backup is turned on for the device, Android may include this save file in the device owner's Google account backup. That backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it.

Children

Star Hatchling is designed for children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the Star Hatchling page in the Google Play Store.
