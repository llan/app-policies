Laser Dragon Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Laser Dragon is a game made for young children. It does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game:

  • has no internet access: it does not ask for the internet permission. The only Android permission it uses is vibration, for a short gentle buzz during play, which a parent can turn off in the game's settings;
  • has no accounts, sign-in, chat, or ways to talk to other people;
  • has no ads and no in-app purchases;
  • has no analytics, tracking, or third-party software that collects data.

What stays on the device

The game keeps its progress in a small save file in its own private storage on the device: each player's profile (a picture of a dragon face, and a name only if a parent chooses to type one; the name is optional), the difficulty, stars and gems earned in play (gems are pretend and cannot be bought), which levels are open, upgrades, dragons and lasers owned, dress-up picks, which parts of the story and tutorial have been seen, and the game's settings (sound and music volume, calm mode, vibration, screen shake, and the second-finger beam). While saving, it may also keep a backup copy, a temporary copy, or a set-aside copy of a damaged save, all in the same private storage on the device. These files never leave the device through the game, and the developer cannot see them.

When the game is uninstalled, Android may ask whether to keep its data on the device, so progress isn't lost by accident. If you choose not to keep it, the save files are deleted.

The game turns off Android's app backup, so the save file is not copied to a Google account backup either.

Children

Laser Dragon is designed for children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the Laser Dragon page in the Google Play Store.
