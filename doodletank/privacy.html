Doodle Tank Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Doodle Tank is a fish tank game made for young children. It does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game:

  • asks for only one Android permission, to make the device buzz gently (this can be turned off in the game's settings); it has no internet access and does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts;
  • has no accounts, sign-in, chat, or ways to talk to other people;
  • has no ads and no in-app purchases;
  • has no analytics, tracking, or third-party software that collects data.

What stays on the device

The game keeps its progress in a save file on the device. The save holds: player profile names typed in the game and the picture chosen for each profile, the difficulty picked, levels finished, medals and shells earned in the game, the animals and decorations in the player's own tank with any names given to them in the game, the day each animal joined the tank (used only to show its age in days), and the game's settings (sound and music volume, calm mode, vibration). Names typed in the game stay on the device. The save file never leaves the device through the game, it is not included in Android's backup, and the developer cannot see it.

Children

Doodle Tank is designed for children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the Doodle Tank page in the Google Play Store.
